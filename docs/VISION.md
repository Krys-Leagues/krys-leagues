# Krys Leagues Vision

## Mission

Build the definitive competitive platform for Walkabout Mini Golf.

Krys Leagues is not simply a website or a Discord bot. It is a complete ecosystem that connects players, leagues, tournaments, statistics, broadcasts, rankings, historical records, and community games into one unified platform.

---

# Core Principles

## One Player. One Identity.

Every player has one permanent profile.

That profile connects:

- League history
- KWT history
- Tournament history
- Community games
- Handicap
- Badges
- Awards
- Records
- Statistics
- Future systems

Names may change.

Discord names may change.

Leagues may change.

Player history never changes.

---

## Preserve History

Nothing is ever discarded.

Historical information is one of the most valuable assets of the platform.

---

## Build Once — Reuse Everywhere

Features should become reusable systems.

A Game Engine should power:

- Casual games
- Leagues
- Tournaments
- Ladders
- Community events

rather than building separate systems for each.

---

## Discord Is Part Of The Platform

Discord is where people interact.

The website is where permanent data lives.

Neither replaces the other.

Both work together.

---

## Community First

Krys Leagues exists to make playing Walkabout Mini Golf easier, more organized, more competitive, and more enjoyable for every level of player.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Create a platform that can continue growing for years without requiring major redesigns by building every new feature on a solid architectural foundation.
