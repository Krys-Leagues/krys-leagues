# Weekly source provenance (Phase 54)

- Source: The Weekly, an external tournament population.
- Original archive: phase52-input/Krys_Handicap_Weekly_Evidence_Package.zip as preserved by extraction.
- Manifest: phase52-input/weekly_manifest.csv.
- Initial package verification: all 56 manifest hashes matched.
- Row-level admitted evidence: weekly_course_evidence.csv, with original screenshot references and displayed row positions.
- Manifest-level classification: weekly_manifest_status.csv.
- Uncertain, unreadable, truncated, superseded, or not-yet-transcribed rows: weekly_evidence_exceptions.csv.
- Weekly course evidence is never eligible for personal Handicap histories or individual Handicap Indexes.
- External source player IDs remain separate; no Krys Leagues accounts or identity merges are created.
- KWT and Mighty Cocobot evidence remain separate.
- Phase 54 visually transcribed readable rows from S21W05 GLE/HWH, S20W05 MGE/ZZH, S20W06 BHE/QVH, and S20W07 ALE/BHH; all omitted source-name rows remain quarantined. Other remaining event groups remain explicitly partial and are not silently promoted to verified.
- BHE and BHH combined KWT+Weekly simulations are withheld after the Weekly samples expanded because the raw KWT score export is not present locally.
